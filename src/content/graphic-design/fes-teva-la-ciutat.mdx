<section class="case-section">
  <h2>The gap is not where you would expect it</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Bicing is Barcelona's public bike-sharing service. The brief was open: pick a brand, propose a campaign, defend it in two weeks."}</p>
    <p>{"Three numbers sit oddly together. Women are the majority of people crossing Barcelona on public transport. They are a shrinking minority in the bike lane. But among Bicing users specifically, the split is close to even."}</p>
  </div>
  <CaseStudyStats
    items={[
      { value: '30.7%', label: 'of people counted cycling in Barcelona in 2025 were women, about five points below 2021' },
      { value: '56 / 67%', label: 'of metro and bus trips in the city are made by women' },
      { value: '72/28 → 54/46', label: 'men to women among people counted cycling, against the split among Bicing users' },
    ]}
  />
  <p class="case-source">{"The two sides of that last comparison are not the same measurement. 72/28 counts people observed cycling in the city on any bike, Bicing included. 54/46 is the split among Bicing users. They come from different datasets and different methods, so read it as indicative of a direction, not as a like-for-like figure. Sources: BACC, Radiografia Ciclista de Barcelona · EMEF / IERMB, enquesta de mobilitat · B:SM / Bicing user data."}</p>
  <div class="case-copy" style="margin-top:clamp(2rem,5vw,3rem);">
    <p>{"So the campaign's job is Bicing ridership among women inside the service area. The wider gap in the bike lane is why the opportunity exists. It is not the number this campaign moves."}</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>She already crosses the city, on fixed lines</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Two thirds of bus trips and more than half of metro trips in Barcelona are made by women, on the EMEF mobility survey. TMB's own client research puts both figures higher still. The mobility is already there. What is missing is the freedom to choose the route rather than follow one."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>What the audience is actually turning down</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"One thing has to be said before anything else. BACC attributes the gap substantially to perceived insecurity on a network riders do not consider complete or safe, and to the harassment women report while riding. That barrier is real, and this campaign does not touch it. Art direction cannot rebuild a bike network."}</p>
    <p>{"What is left is the part a campaign can reach: the commitment and the identity that come attached to owning a bike. From here the project runs on a hypothesis rather than a finding. The reading is that what gets turned down is not the bicycle but everything bolted onto it, the gear, the clothes, the posture, the idea that riding means becoming a cyclist."}</p>
    <p>{"That is also why the public bike matters. You do not have to own it, store it, maintain it or identify with it. You take one, you leave it."}</p>
    <p class="case-source" style="margin-top:1.25rem;">{"Two weeks allowed desk research only. No interviews, no survey, no intercepts. Everything above the data is a reading, not a measured finding."}</p>
  </div>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Who the campaign is aimed at</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Not a demographic. A behaviour. She is a regular bus or metro user inside the service area, she can already ride, she makes several short trips a week, and she defaults to public transport because cycling feels like a bigger commitment than the trip warrants."}</p>
    <p>{"She is a composite rather than a person. Her day is not a line. It is work, the pool, and a plan that moves at half past six. Today she fits her route to the transport available. Bicing can fit itself to her route instead."}</p>
  </div>
  <div class="case-route-figure">
    <CaseStudyRealRoute items={['Home', '', 'Work', '', 'Pool', '', 'Dinner', '']} />
  </div>
  <p class="case-figcaption">{"One line, redrawn for every journey. It never holds the same shape twice."}</p>

  <div class="case-objectives">
    <article>
      <span>01</span>
      <p>{"Do not turn her into a cyclist. Fit Bicing into the day she already has."}</p>

    <article>

      <p>{"Make Bicing one more ordinary way of crossing Barcelona, not an activity reserved for people who call themselves cyclists."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>A system borrowed from the street</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Barcelona already has a visual language for getting around: the signage, the roundels, Helvetica Neue on red. A new one would put Bicing in competition with the modes it should be sitting next to. So the system borrows instead of inventing."}</p>
    <p>{"Four elements. The module comes from the Bicing mark. The grid repeats at the rhythm of an Eixample block, chamfered corners and streets included. The station is the service as it appears in the street. The red line is the route."}</p>
    <p>{"Borrowing has a cost, and it is the whole risk in this project. Red plus Helvetica plus transport information reads as TMB before it reads as Bicing. The route line is what has to carry the difference, so it gets a rule of its own."}</p>
  </div>
  <dl class="case-rule">
    <dt>The rule</dt>
    <dd>{"The line runs dock to dock, it turns only on the chamfer angle of an Eixample block, and it is drawn once per trip from that trip's own stops. A transit line repeats because it has to. This one cannot: every journey produces a different shape, and the shape is the point."}</dd>
  </dl>
  <p class="case-large-copy" style="margin-top:clamp(2rem,5vw,3rem);">{"The metro line is fixed. The Bicing line is yours."}</p>
  <CaseStudySystemDiagram />
  <p class="case-figcaption">{"Four elements, and the line that carries between them."}</p>
</section>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Colour and type do the sorting</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Red is reserved for the route and for anything belonging to Bicing. Everything else stays asphalt black, white and infrastructure grey, so the route is always the thing that reads first."}</p>
    <p>{"Helvetica Neue is already the typeface of Barcelona's transport signage. Bold carries the claims, regular carries station names and times. Nothing here needs to be invented for the campaign to look like it belongs."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Applications</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">

  </div>
  <CaseStudyPoster />

I have kept the poster as it was presented rather than redraw it here."}</p>
  <div class="case-copy" style="margin-top:clamp(2.5rem,6vw,4rem);">

<section class="case-section case-unresolved">
  <h2>Where this goes next</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Two weeks, no results, no testing. Three things would need answering before this went any further."}</p>
  </div>
  <ol class="case-unresolved-list">
    <li>{"Bus, metro and Bicing all lead with red. Borrowing the street's language is the premise, and this is the bill for it. The route line rule is the proposed answer, and it has not been proved."}</li>
    <li>{"The stop and time format was designed on a screen. A bus shelter gets about two seconds."}</li>
    <li>{"Nothing here has been tested with the audience it describes."}</li>
  </ol>
  <div class="case-next">
    <h3>What another two weeks would buy</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>{"Specify the route line rule properly and draw it against a TMB layout, to see whether it survives standing next to one."}</li>
      <li>{"Test the poster at two seconds with five people, and cut whatever does not survive."}</li>
      <li>{"Three more applications, to prove the format carries beyond a poster and a bike panel."}</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

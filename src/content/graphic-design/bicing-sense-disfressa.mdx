<section class="case-section">
  <h2>The gap is not where you would expect it</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Bicing is Barcelona's public bike-sharing service. The brief was open: pick a brand, propose a campaign, defend it in two weeks."}</p>
    <p>{"The proposal starts from three numbers that sit oddly together. Women are the majority of people crossing Barcelona on public transport. They are a shrinking minority in the bike lane. But on Bicing specifically, the gap is much smaller than it is on privately owned bikes."}</p>
  </div>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>She already crosses the city, on fixed lines</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Two thirds of bus trips and more than half of metro trips in Barcelona are made by women. The mobility is already there. What is missing is the freedom to choose the route rather than follow one."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>What the audience is actually turning down</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"The gap is not about skill or willingness. Most of the women in the research already know how to ride. What they turn down is everything attached to it: the gear, the clothes, the posture, the idea that using a bike means becoming a cyclist."}</p>
    <p>{"That is also why the public bike matters. You do not have to own it, store it, maintain it or identify with it. You take one, you leave it."}</p>
  </div>

<section class="case-insight">
  <blockquote>
    They do not reject the bicycle. They reject having to become cyclists in order to use it.
  </blockquote>
  <p>{"The line the proposal is built on, translated from the original."}</p>
</section>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>The L5 rider</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"She is 25 to 44. She lives, works or studies inside the Bicing service area. She already moves around the city and she knows how to ride. She does not think of herself as a cyclist."}</p>
    <p>{"Her day is not a line. It is home, work, the gym, family, friends, an errand, and a plan that moves at half past six. Today she fits her route to the transport available. Bicing can fit itself to her route instead."}</p>
  </div>
  <div class="case-route-figure">
    <CaseStudyRealRoute items={['Home', 'Work', 'Gym', 'Friends', 'Family', 'Health', 'Leisure', 'Home']} />
  </div>
  <p class="case-figcaption">{"The route graphic from the proposal. One flexible line, drawn once per journey, never the same shape twice."}</p>

  <div class="case-objectives">
    <article>
      <span>01</span>
      <p>{"Do not turn her into a cyclist. Fit Bicing into the day she already has."}</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>02</span>
      <p>{"Win the regular trips, and the changes of plan."}</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>03</span>
      <p>{"Make Bicing one more ordinary way of crossing Barcelona, not an activity reserved for people who call themselves cyclists."}</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>A system borrowed from the street</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Barcelona already has a visual language for getting around: the signage, the roundels, Helvetica Neue on red. A new one would put Bicing in competition with the modes it should be sitting next to. So the system borrows instead of inventing."}</p>
    <p>{"Four elements. The module comes from the Bicing mark. The grid repeats at the rhythm of an Eixample block, chamfered corners and streets included. The station is the service as it appears in the street. The red line is the route, and it changes shape every time."}</p>
  </div>
  <CaseStudySystemDiagram />
  <p class="case-figcaption">{"The four system elements, rebuilt from the original artwork. The route line is what carries between them."}</p>
</section>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Colour and type do the sorting</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Red is reserved for the route and for anything belonging to Bicing. Everything else stays asphalt black, white and infrastructure grey, so the route is always the thing that reads first."}</p>
    <p>{"Helvetica Neue is already the typeface of Barcelona's transport signage. Bold carries the claims, regular carries station names and times. Nothing here needs to be invented for the campaign to look like it belongs."}</p>
  </div>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Photography without the costume</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Women dressed for where they are going, not for a ride. Natural light and real contrast, including traffic lights, stations and night. No posing, no eye contact, no hero framing. The bicycle stays a tool and never becomes the subject."}</p>
    <p>{"The frames below are references from the proposal. The shoot itself would happen in Barcelona."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Copy that describes a moment, not a benefit</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"The lines stay in Catalan, stay short, and read closer to a notification than a slogan. They talk about time and small decisions. The claim closes the idea rather than explaining it."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Applications</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"The poster names the two stops and the time of the trip it belongs to, so the route on the image is a specific journey rather than decoration. The same system runs on the front panel of the bikes, where the rider chooses between electric and mechanical."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>What is unresolved</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"This was a two week course project, so there are no campaign results behind it. Three things would need answering before it went any further."}</p>
  </div>
  <ol class="case-unresolved-list">
    <li>{"The stop and time format was designed on a screen. A bus shelter gets about two seconds."}</li>
    <li>{"Bus, metro and Bicing all lead with red. The campaign may need a second identifier to stay recognisably Bicing."}</li>
    <li>{"Nothing here has been tested with the audience it describes."}</li>
  </ol>

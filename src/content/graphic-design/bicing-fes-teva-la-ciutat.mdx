<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Barcelona's bike lane is losing women.</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Women made up 30.7% of observed cycle-lane users in 2025. In 2021, the figure was 35.6%."}</p>
    <p>{"Women also make up 68% of bus passengers and 60% of metro passengers. They already cross the city every day."}</p>
    <p>{"One 2023 BACC cycling-profile sample split 72/28. A 2020 Bicing user survey split 53.8/46.2. The public service is much closer to parity."}</p>
  </div>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>The L5 user</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"She is 25 to 44 and lives, works or studies inside the Bicing service area. She knows how to ride."}</p>
    <p>{"Her day includes home, work, the pool, her mother's place and dinner. At half past six, it changes."}</p>
    <p>{"A metro line follows one line. Her day doesn't."}</p>
  </div>
  <CaseStudyRoute items={['Home', 'Work', 'Gym', 'Relationships', 'Family', 'Health', 'Leisure']} />
  <p class="case-route-caption">{"Her day does not fit on one line."}</p>

  <div class="case-objectives">
    <article>
      <span>01</span>
      <p>{"Don't make her a cyclist. Put Bicing inside the day she already has."}</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>02</span>
      <p>{"Win the awkward legs. The transfer, the detour, the change of plan."}</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>03</span>
      <p>{"Make it ordinary. A public bike becomes part of the street."}</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>One structure. Different routes across it.</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"The module comes from the Bicing mark. Repeated, it works like a city-block grid. One red line crosses it."}</p>
    <p>{"The line carries the route. Everything else stays asphalt black, white or infrastructure grey."}</p>
    <p>{"Helvetica Neue already speaks through Barcelona's signage. Bold carries claims. Regular handles station names and times."}</p>
  </div>

<section class="case-section">
  <h2>Everyday movement, no costume.</h2>
  <div class="case-copy">
    <p>{"Women are dressed for where they're going. Natural light, real contrast, traffic lights, stations and night."}</p>
    <p>{"No posing or eye contact. The bicycle stays a tool and never becomes the subject."}</p>
    <p>{"The reference frames below are placeholders. The proposed shoot would take place in Barcelona."}</p>

<section class="case-section case-unresolved">
  <h2>What I'd fix next</h2>
  <p class="case-large-copy">{"This is a course project, so there are no campaign results behind it. Three questions still need answers."}</p>
  <ol class="case-unresolved-list">
    <li>{"Does the station-and-time format read as useful information in the two seconds someone gives a shelter poster?"}</li>

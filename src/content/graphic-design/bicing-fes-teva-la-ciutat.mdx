<section class="case-section">

  <h2>Fewer women are choosing the bike lane.</h2>
  <div class="case-two-up align-end">
    <div>
      <p>{"Women make most journeys on Barcelona's public transport, yet they remain a minority among people cycling. The gap suggests that the city is already being travelled—it is the mode, its perceived safety and its cultural identity that create friction."}</p>
      <p>{"The research pointed to an important distinction: private cycling is strongly male-coded, while Bicing's shared, public character brings participation much closer to parity."}</p>

<section class="case-section">
  <p class="case-section-number">{"04 · Strategy"}</p>
  <h2>Make Bicing a tool, not an identity.</h2>
  <div class="case-objectives">
    <article>
      <span>01</span>
      <p>{"Do not turn her into a cyclist. Introduce Bicing into the day she already has."}</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>02</span>
      <p>{"Make it useful for familiar journeys, spontaneous detours and changing plans."}</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>03</span>
      <p>{"Normalize public bicycles as part of Barcelona's everyday transport landscape."}</p>
    </article>
  </div>

<section class="case-section case-concept">
  <p class="case-section-number">{"05 · Creative concept"}</p>
  <p class="case-concept-prelude">{"A public bicycle."}<br/>{"A route of your own."}</p>
  <h2>Bicing.<br/><em>Fes teva la ciutat.</em></h2>
  <p>{"“Make the city yours” is demonstrated through ordinary decisions rather than explained as an abstract promise: half an hour before dinner, a route through the park, or a last-minute change of plans."}</p>
</section>

<section class="case-section case-reflection">
  <p class="case-section-number">{"09 · Reflection"}</p>
  <h2>A campaign built around permission, not persuasion.</h2>
  <p>{"The proposal avoids asking women to adopt the codes of cycling culture. Instead, it gives an existing public service a more useful role in the life they already lead. The next step would be to test whether the language and route-based applications improve relevance, perceived safety and trial among non-cyclist Bicing prospects."}</p>
  <p>{"As an academic concept, the project demonstrates the strategic and visual direction rather than reporting live campaign results."}</p>
</section>

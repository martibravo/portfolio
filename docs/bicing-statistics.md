# Bicing case study: statistics audit

Reviewed 7 September 2026 against the original presentation, slides 4–10.

| Original claim | Finding and correction for the original slides |
| --- | --- |
| Slide 5: 30.7% of cyclists are women; down 5% since 2021 | BACC’s combined bicycle/scooter observation series is 30.70% versus 35.60%: down 4.9 percentage points. These are passages at six sites in selected peak periods, with visually estimated gender, not unique riders or a citywide census. Bicycle-only figures are separately 30.73% versus 35.31%, excluding scooters and cargo bikes. A lower share does not establish fewer women riding. |
| Slide 7: women make 56% of metro trips and 67% of bus trips | Could not substantiate the undated EMEF attribution. Replaced with TMB’s 2022 customer profile: women represent 63% of metro users and 74% of TMB bus users. These describe users, not trip shares. |
| Slide 9: private bicycles 72/28 versus Bicing 54/46 in 2025 | Municipal 2022 figure of 28% covers bicycle AND personal mobility vehicles, not private bikes. Could not verify 46% as a 2025 Bicing figure; removed comparison. Older secondary reporting of subscriber gender is insufficient to establish the claimed year or comparable population. |
| Slide 10: cycling identity explains the gap | Creative hypothesis, not a causal conclusion of these statistics. Explicitly labelled as pending audience validation. |

## Primary sources

- [BACC, Radiografia Ciclista de Barcelona 2025](https://bacc.cat/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2025_Informe-Radiografia-Ciclista-de-Barcelona_CAT.pdf): February 2026 publication; methods pp. 10–11, gender series pp. 20–21. Fieldwork September–October 2025.
- [TMB, customer profile](https://noticies.tmb.cat/sala-de-premsa/dones-de-fins-35-anys-residents-barcelona-perfil-de-persones-usuaries-de-bus-metro): published 4 May 2023, reporting the 2022 customer-perception study.
- [Barcelona PMU 2030, session 12](https://www.barcelona.cat/mobilitat/sites/default/files/2024-06/240618_PMU2030_S12_Mobilitat%20metropolitana_V4.pdf): 18 June 2024, p. 11; chart sources Ajuntament, EMEF and TMB (2022).
- [Institut Metròpoli, women and everyday cycling](https://www.institutmetropoli.cat/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Enquesta_Bici_Dona_AMB.pdf): 2023 survey, published 2024. Harassment discussed pp. 57–58; does not prove the identity hypothesis.

The website uses the corrected figures. The downloadable PDF is the untouched original 57-slide presentation, accompanied by a source-verification warning beside its download link. The table above documents the changes to make in the editable presentation; they have not been applied to the PDF. The student campaign is not presented as commissioned or validated work. Different years and populations provide context, not a controlled modal comparison.
